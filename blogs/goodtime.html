Origin

The 10th Grade entrance exam before the education reform in HCMC was something else, it's the first obstacle and torment to children cause how competitive it is to get into a public high school. However the later generation wouldn't feel the same way due to the education reform by MOET.

2024 is the year I took the exam, I wasn't good at mathematics and literature at that moment so I did the tests badly but thanks god the english test was easy and feasible, it carried me a lot and got me a decent school, Go Vap High School.

However I was traumatized by that result; it took me months to recover from that. Go Vap High School (formerly known as "Chan Phuoc Liem private High School") is a decent high school with basic infrastucture; I really don't expect anything good from it. Nonetheless, I fell in love with its elegant atmosphere; studying in a class from final floor, I was gifted a stunning, dazzling view.

Imperfect hatred

Good class, good school, but great people? Absolutely not.

After learning in this school for a year, I can say that most people are pure hostile, childish dickheads. The way they judge me, like I was just an immature child and they act like they're adults. Most people didn't see the image of bullying in this school because it's vaguely up and down.

Perhaps they thought I was soft-hearted and silly person for being funny, but my main goal of becoming a tomfoolery is just engagement. I hate my image when I was a 11th grade student, I was a kid who crave for attention. However, I hate it now.

Do they really think they're right and funny? Or they're proud of winning over somebody? Was the victory worth it?

Emotional detachment

Upon reaching senior year, I wasn't the same person. I learned how to become a "Grey Rock" in front of non-constructive criticism, my classmates keep mumbling the same corny nonsense over and over again to harass me when I tried to make fun.

"Hypocrisy, Double standard, fragile ego.", they told me.

I believe they knew zilch about those words (they told me i'm a hypocrite for defending a mentally ill person from being called "Down"). Also they use the term "Down" to refer everybody who are either being mentally ill or making a mistake. Pretty disgusting.

But thanks to those arrogant people, I can learn how to manage my temper, mentality and ego vigilantly at same time thus it sometimes refrain me from ranting.

Conclusion

I love and hate my class at same time but I can judge who they are, humans are complex and you can't even predict a person. Who know who will my classmates become in future? a successful person or a stone that can't see a glint of light before their 30s?
